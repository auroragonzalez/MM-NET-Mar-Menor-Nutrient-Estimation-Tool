Data Sources

  • Ramblas MARMENOR (IMIDA/DGMM): Weekly grab samples of NO3 and PO4 concentration at 23 control points (14 after eliminating those with less than 5 measurements), together with weekly streamflow estimates, spanning August 2019 to March 2026.
  • SAIH–CHS: 5-minute resolution records of streamflow (Q), precipitation, and water level at 12 gauging and pluviometric stations throughout the catchment from 2016–present.
  • AEMET: Hourly meteorological data from four terrestrial stations (Cartagena, Fuente Álamo, San Javier, Torre Pacheco), covering 2023–2025.
  • SIAM: Daily agro-meteorological records from five stations (CA12, CA73, TP22, TP42, TP91) with continuous coverage from 2016 to 2026, used to extend the precipitation record beyond the AEMET window.
